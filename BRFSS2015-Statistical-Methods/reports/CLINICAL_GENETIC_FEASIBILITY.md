# Clinical/genetic feasibility

CDC codebook availability scan preserved in evidence/gap_extension/clinical_genetic_availability.json. DIABETE3 is self-reported diagnosis; PDIABTST/BLDSUGAR/INSULIN describe reported testing, monitoring or treatment rather than measured glucose/HbA1c/genotypes. UCI abstract claims of laboratory features are inconsistent with its21-variable schema and are not accepted as laboratory evidence.

Genetic/laboratory validation is INFEASIBLE_WITH_AVAILABLE_DATA. External clinical data and appropriate approvals/linkage would be required; no invented variables or biological-negative classifications.
